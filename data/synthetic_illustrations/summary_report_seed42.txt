      Scale: Coverage ratio (fraction of conditions shown to independently affect the decision outcome)
  (b) SOTIF triggering condition coverage in scenario testing (ISO 21448)
      Scale: Coverage fraction (identified triggering conditions covered by scenario tests)
  (c) Deep ensemble disagreement as uncertainty indicator (ISO/PAS 8800)
      Scale: AUROC, a scalar in [0, 1] (separation of correct from incorrect detections)
  (d) Adversarial penetration testing against LiDAR spoofing and perturbation (ISO/SAE 21434)
      Scale: Attack success rate under the threat model

DP-2: the evidence types are disjoint by design, each on its own
measurement scale, and no standard prescribes how they combine
into a single sufficiency claim at G5.
