Assurance gaps in an integrated safety and cybersecurity case

Milin Patel and Rolf Jung, Kempten University of Applied Sciences. Supplementary material for a WAISE 2026 workshop paper and a SAFECOMP 2026 position paper.

An AI-based perception component for highly automated driving falls within the scope of four standards at once, and each prescribes its own evidence. The WAISE paper combines their claims in one GSN argument, a structure no standard prescribes, and finds that at the verification and validation goal (G5) the four evidence types are on different measurement scales and no standard prescribes how they combine. The position paper finds a corresponding gap in operation: no clause assigns a runtime anomaly to a concern, or resolves the concern-specific results into one judgement.
